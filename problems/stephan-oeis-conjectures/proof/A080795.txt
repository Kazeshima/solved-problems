A080795 and A006154: a proof that  A080795(2n) = 2^n A006154(2n)

26 November 2026.

Provenance.  The proof was found and written by Claude, an AI model made by
Anthropic, and then formalised in Lean 4 (see Section 3).  The text has not
been peer reviewed.


1. STATEMENT

Let a(n) be OEIS A080795, the number of minimax trees on n nodes (Foata and
Han [FH]), and let b(n) be OEIS A006154, the number of ordered partitions of
an n-set into blocks of odd size:

    a :  1, 1, 4, 20, 128, 1024, 9856, 110720, ...
    b :  1, 1, 2,  7,  32,  181, 1232,   9787, ...

R. Stephan conjectured in 2004 that a(2n) = 2^n b(2n); this is Conjecture 85
in [S], and the formula line in A080795 is still marked "conjectured".

THEOREM.  For every n >= 0,   a(2n) = 2^n b(2n).

The two sequences enter only through the recurrences stated in their OEIS
entries:

    a(0) = 1,   a(n+1) = 2 sum_{k=0..n} binomial(n,k) a(k) a(n-k) - 0^n;
    b(0) = 1,   b(n+1) = sum_{j odd, 1 <= j <= n+1} binomial(n+1,j) b(n+1-j).

They express the obvious decompositions.  In a minimax tree on n+1 >= 2
nodes the root carries the least or the greatest label (2 choices) and the
remaining n labels are distributed over the left and the right subtree,
either of which may be empty.  In an ordered partition into odd blocks the
first block is an odd-sized subset and the rest is again such a partition.


2. PROOF

Let A(x) = sum a(n) x^n / n! and B(x) = sum b(n) x^n / n!, as formal power
series.  The recurrences say

    A' = 2 A^2 - 1,  A(0) = 1;        B = 1 + sinh(x) B.

Put w = sqrt(2), P = exp(w x), M = exp(-w x), and

    U = (1 - w) P + (1 + w) M,        V = (2 - w) P + (2 + w) M.

Since w^2 = 2 we have U' = -V and V' = -2U.

Step 1:  2 A U = V.  Let Q = 2 A U - V.  Then

    Q' = 2 (2A^2 - 1) U - 2 A V + 2 U = 2 A (2 A U - V) = 2 A Q,

and Q(0) = 2 * 2 - 4 = 0.  A power series with Q' = G Q and Q(0) = 0 is
zero: if its coefficients up to x^m vanish, then so does the coefficient of
x^m in G Q, which is (m+1) times the coefficient of x^(m+1) in Q.  (This is
the usual linearisation of a Riccati equation: A = -U'/(2U), U'' = 2U.)

Step 2:  the even part of A.  Replacing x by -x exchanges P and M, hence
turns U, V into U~ = (1-w) M + (1+w) P and V~ = (2-w) M + (2+w) P, and
2 A(-x) U~ = V~.  Using P M = 1 and w^2 = 2,

    U U~ = 6 - P^2 - M^2,        V U~ + V~ U = 16.

Therefore  (A(x) + A(-x)) * 2 (6 - P^2 - M^2) = 16.

Step 3:  the even part of B.  Substituting w x for x in B (1 - sinh x) = 1
gives B(wx) (2 - P + M) = 2, and then B(-wx) (2 - M + P) = 2.  Since
(2 - P + M)(2 + P - M) = 4 - (P - M)^2 = 6 - P^2 - M^2,

    (B(wx) + B(-wx)) * 2 (6 - P^2 - M^2) = 16.

Step 4.  The series 2 (6 - P^2 - M^2) has constant term 8, so it can be
cancelled:  A(x) + A(-x) = B(wx) + B(-wx).  The coefficient of x^(2n) is
2 a(2n)/(2n)! on the left and 2 w^(2n) b(2n)/(2n)! = 2 * 2^n b(2n)/(2n)! on
the right.  []

In closed form, both sides of Step 4 equal 4 / (3 - cosh(2 sqrt(2) x)), that
is, twice 1 / (1 - sinh^2(sqrt(2) x)).

There is no such relation for odd indices: a(3) = 20 and b(3) = 7.


3. REMARKS

Formal verification.  The Theorem, for the sequences defined by the two
recurrences above, is MinimaxTrees.a_even in ../MinimaxTrees.lean (Lean 4
with Mathlib, formal power series over the reals, no axioms beyond the three
standard ones).  The combinatorial descriptions are not formalised; the
script ../minimax/verify_identity.py enumerates minimax trees and ordered
partitions into odd blocks for n <= 7 and compares the counts with the
recurrences, and checks the identity up to 2n = 400.

Earlier work.  Both generating functions are in the OEIS entries (the
differential equation was recorded there by P. Bala in 2011), so the
identity is a short computation once it is set up as above.  A web search
found no written proof.
