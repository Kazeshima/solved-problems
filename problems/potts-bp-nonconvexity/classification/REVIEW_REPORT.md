## Corrections and proof checks

1. **Admissibility.** The earlier `sourceImage` totalizes division by zero; the published counterexample parameters had independently proved positive normalization. At $\beta=0,d\ge q$, pinning every colour gives zero raw weights. The legacy image then equals $\Delta_q\cup\{0\}$, which is nonconvex. The problem expressly excludes these inputs. The new `admissibleSourceImage` evaluates the original finite expectation with an explicit positive-total condition. It equals `generalImage`, and for every $0\le\beta\le1$ equals the legacy `sourceImage` with the zero vector removed. For positive $\beta$ and $q>0$ it equals the entire legacy image. These are theorems, with an additional `legacy_zero_image` proving the precise zero-temperature discrepancy. [Source definitions](../PottsBPSource.lean) · [New equivalences and final statements](../PottsClassification.lean)
2. **A small flaw in the proposed transfer justification.** An arbitrary chosen larger entry of a pair of shifted counts need not be at least 1 when three entries sum to 2. For $d=4$, $b=(2,0,0)$ and actual counts $(0,0,4)$, the shifted counts are $(-2,0,4)$. Transferring one unit from the second coordinate (shifted value 0) to the first is infeasible. Choosing the *global* maximum and minimum repairs the text because the global maximum is at least 1. The integer tangent inequality in our proof eliminates the choice of transfers altogether. This is a proof-detail correction and does not change the theorem.
3. **The triangular face.** The lower bound is strict if any inactive colour occurs or any shifted active count is outside $\{0,1\}$. All three equality signatures are feasible for every $d\ge2$. Their raw vectors are linearly independent even after positive rescaling. The proposed relative-interior point normalizes their raw mean. In general it is a convex combination of the normalized vertices with coefficients proportional to their normalizers. Equal weights on the normalized vertices are unnecessary. The argument for Cartesian supports is correct. The Lean proof uses the stronger single-column tangent and its interior-cell rigidity. [Column lemma](../PottsClassColumn.lean) · [Nonconvexity](../PottsClassHot.lean)
4. **The polygon, closed lift and winding.** Every genuine supporting line has coefficients of both signs, and a minimizing pure signature occupies only positive-coefficient colours. Strict second differences leave at most two consecutive minimizers. The converse edge calculation exposes all $3d$ edges. Each edge has only its two distinct endpoint signatures, so distinct edges intersect only at their common vertices. Successive single-row moves return to the same *labelled* product tuple. The image traverses each edge once, yielding winding number of absolute value 1 about every interior point. The loop contracts upstairs in the convex domain, and throughout $\beta>0$ its image is defined. Thus the proposed winding proof is valid. For kernel verification we instead supplied the continuous fixed-ratio interpolation in the proof. Only the ordinary one-dimensional intermediate value theorem is needed. The explicit count of all edges and the winding description are pen-and-paper statements; the kernel checks the edge inequalities, attainable edge crossings, fibre filling and resulting convexity. [Edges](../PottsClassEdges.lean) · [Interpolation](../PottsClassFiber.lean) · [Ternary image](../PottsClassThree.lean)
5. **Zero-temperature inverse.** The draft's positive pivot and separate point-mass case work. Choosing a maximum pivot instead puts all scaled coordinates in $[0,1]$, including point masses. The endpoint inequality may be an equality, which the IVT permits. The root cannot be zero. Thus the output is defined even when some target coordinates vanish. [Machine-checked star construction](../PottsClassZero.lean)
6. **Domain and source quantifiers.** Each of the $d$ rows independently ranges over all real nonnegative vectors of sum 1. No identical-distribution or full-support condition is present. In `paper_classification`, the matrix entries, product configuration masses, sum over all $q^d$ configurations, normalization guard and Euclidean convexity are displayed directly. This independent statement elaborates to the iff by definitional equality. At $\beta=1$, $d=1$ and the boundary values, no use of a positive-temperature limiting theorem replaces a direct argument. [Unfolded theorem](../PottsClassification.lean)
