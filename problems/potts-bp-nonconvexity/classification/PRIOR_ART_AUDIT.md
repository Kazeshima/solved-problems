# Prior-art audit for the full classification

**Search date: 25 November 2026. Priority verdict: INCONCLUSIVE.** I found the existing published partial theorem, the source's binary observation, and several closely related results about tree recursions. I did not find an earlier proof of the full classification, of nonconvexity for all $q\ge4,d\ge2$ at positive temperature, of the exact ternary image polygon, or of the zero-temperature full-simplex threshold. The last three findings describe this search's coverage, not a priority certificate.

## Criterion for duplication

For the equivalent Hadamard formulation, a single normalized row ranges over the truncated simplex $\{u\in\Delta_q:u_i\le1/(q-1+\beta)\}$ when $\beta<1$. Indeed $u_i=(1-(1-\beta)p_i)/(q-1+\beta)$ and the lower bound $u_i\ge\beta/(q-1+\beta)$ follows from the other upper bounds. Normalize the coordinatewise product of $d$ independently chosen members of this one fixed simplex. This exact constraint matters when comparing with arbitrary credal sets or projective algebraic varieties.

The object is the exact attainable set after **one normalized update**, over arbitrary independent, potentially different child marginals on the real closed simplex. Equivalent work could describe a normalized Hadamard power of the simplex of interaction vectors, equality of product-law outputs with their deterministic convex hull, or attainability of every prescribed proper-colouring root marginal. A general theorem covering the same objects and parameters would count even without Potts terminology. Bounds on attainable points, convex supersets, log-convex invariant regions, algebraic closures and asymptotic uniqueness require a separate implication to decide this Euclidean image question.

| Component | Evidence and assessment |
|---|---|
| $\beta=1$, $d=1$, two colours | The constant map and the affine one-row image follow immediately from the definition. The binary connected-interval argument is explicitly present in Liu–Mani–Pernice, Section 4. These are elementary or pre-existing observations. |
| $q\ge d+2$, including $\beta=0$ | **PREEXISTING.** Kazeshima's earlier note proves this entire family; it already disproved the universally affirmative answer to Question 4. Claiming either as new here would duplicate the note. |
| $0<\beta<1$, $q\ge4$, unrestricted $d\ge2$ | The new part is $d\ge q-1$. The earlier note's disjointness of all supports gives no witness there. The integer-shifted triangular face persists for unlimited occupancy. No equivalent public extension located. |
| $q=3$, $0<\beta<1$ | The deterministic hull observation is available from Lemma A.1, but does not put its interior into the product image. Neither the three-chain edge characterization plus filling nor an equivalent exact image theorem was located. |
| $\beta=0$, $d\ge q-1$ | Availability of a proper root colour or a root marginal bound is weaker than realizing each prescribed point of $\Delta_q$. No earlier explicit inverse or general surjectivity theorem applicable here was located. |
| Complete iff | No matched result combines all regimes. The author's supplied working draft preceded this review and already proposes the classification, the shifted face, the closed boundary loop and the zero-temperature star construction. The independent reconstruction/fixed-ratio interpolation and Lean verification do not imply independent priority over that draft. |

## Primary comparisons

- **Liu, Mani and Pernice**, v1 10 November 2024, v2 8 February 2025. Definition 2 distinguishes the two images, Lemma A.1 states the convex-hull identity, and Lemma 2.2/Remark 3 use an extremal coordinate and its equality conditions for an auxiliary matrix. Theorem 1.4 requires $d\ge2q$ and an occupancy-tail condition. Section 4 explicitly poses Question 4 and gives the binary observation. The auxiliary-matrix argument supplies methodological precedent. No positive-temperature classification for the standard antiferromagnetic matrix occurs there. [v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.06541v2)
- **Mani**, May 2025. Definition 3.9.2 and Question 3.9.18 on p. 163 give the corresponding thesis question. The catalog's Q4813 and its follow-up PR are references to that question and the already known negative answer. Neither is an independent proof of the entire classification. [Thesis](https://dspace.mit.edu/handle/1721.1/159909) · [Q4813 follow-up](https://github.com/10m-u/open-problems-catalog/pull/7)
- **Kazeshima**, 25 November 2026, immutable commit 22864a3858ae02109d70c5743de1ec145113cc0b. Theorem 1 assumes $q\ge d+2$. Its normalizer bound and unused colour give disjoint supports for all neighbours. The new proof uses a shifted bound that allows $d-2$ pinned occurrences. [Previous note and Lean source](https://github.com/Kazeshima/solved-problems/tree/22864a3858ae02109d70c5743de1ec145113cc0b/problems/potts-bp-nonconvexity)
- **Galanis, Goldberg and Yang**, 10 April 2018 preprint. Their three-state tree result studies sensitivity to boundary conditions and uniqueness. The recursion is closely related; the conclusion controls iterates rather than filling the one-step deterministic hull. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.03514) · [Journal publication](https://doi.org/10.1214/18-EJP211)
- **de Boer, Buys and Regts**, November 2020 preprint, revised January 2023. The four-state regular-tree uniqueness proof uses log-convex regions. In the v3 PDF the convex hulls on pp. 9 and 14 enter geometric estimates, and p. 16 discusses a log-convex hull in ratio coordinates. These are distinct from the exact normalized product image with arbitrary independent inputs. **Bencs, de Boer, Buys and Regts**, journal publication August 2023, study uniqueness at large degree. [Four-state paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05638v3) · [Large-degree paper](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10955-023-03145-z)
- **Wu, Xu and Yang**, 24 November 2026. This particularly close-in-date preprint announces a full classification of Gibbs uniqueness on the regular tree, with the zero-temperature exception $q=d+1$. It constructs a convex potential that decreases under recursion. Convexity of a potential is not a characterization of all attainable one-step marginals. **Song, Zhou and Gu**, 2026 ICML AI4Math workshop poster, similarly study a one-step polynomial Lyapunov potential for three-state uniqueness on the infinite binary tree. The workshop page challenged the browser, while its search API returned the authors, abstract and venue. [Sharp uniqueness threshold](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.32635v1) · [Workshop record](https://openreview.net/forum?id=UmBYp5HEf7) · [Retrieved records](results/related-records.json)
- **Mooij and Kappen**, NeurIPS 2008. Their marginal bounds propagate convex sets containing true marginals. A convex outer bound does not imply that every point in it is attainable. **Bocci, Carlini and Kileel** (2015) and **Friedenberg, Oneto and Williams** (2017) study Hadamard products of algebraic varieties, including linear spaces. Algebraic closure loses the real nonnegative inequalities deciding the triangular obstruction here. [Marginal bounds](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2008/file/a3d68b461bd9d3533ee1dd3ce4628ed4-Paper.pdf) · [Linear spaces](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.04301) · [Algebraic varieties](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.03191)
