The graph6 stream in graph10c.g6.xz is Brendan D. McKay's catalog of
connected simple graphs on ten vertices, redistributed under CC BY 4.0.
The published gzip file was losslessly recompressed as XZ. The graph6 rows,
their order, and their LF terminators are preserved.
