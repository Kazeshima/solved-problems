| [Contextuality of real Pauli quadrics](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/) | Li, Gupta and Ganesh asked for a conceptual proof of the four-qubit geometry and for the limiting violated fraction. Muller et al. observed the finite geometry and witness, and Brent W. Jonah supplied the five-qubit degree and certificate data. [Open problems](https://github.com/BrianLi009/four-qubit-hyperbolic-quadric/blob/44a37dbd55029eec23f8c15f071e263984635444/main.tex#L927-L932) · [Jonah’s deposit](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23510634) | Lean proves one four-qubit Clifford orbit, **61,440** optimal assignments and **240** violated-line sets, each isomorphic to $DW(5,2)$. The five-qubit degree is **6,975**, and the general-rank violated fraction tends to **1/2**. [Four-qubit theorem](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/lean/Quadric/ClaimB.lean#L59) · [Five-qubit theorem](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/lean/Quadric/Five/Exact.lean#L45) · [Kernel replay](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/logs/claim-c-final-replay.txt) |
| [Exact finite Shellsort minimax](problems/shellsort-minimax/) | Ying Wai Lee established the finite optima through 16 keys. Mike Dokken (mkdokken) published the candidate values and ties for the larger sizes. Lean proves the attained fixed-table maximum and certificate-checker soundness. [Paper](problems/shellsort-minimax/paper.pdf) · [All minimizing tables](problems/shellsort-minimax/REVIEW.md) · [Formal scope](problems/shellsort-minimax/PROOF_STATUS.md) |
