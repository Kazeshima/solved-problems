| [Flips between zonotope tilings in codimension three](problems/stephan-oeis-conjectures/) | Matthieu Latapy computed the sequence, and Ralf Stephan observed its closed form as Conjecture 69 of his 2004 list. [OEIS A060621](https://oeis.org/A060621) · [Stephan's list](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0409509) | For every $d\ge0$ the tilings of the cyclic zonotope $Z(d+3,d)$ are joined by exactly $(d+3)(d+8)2^{d-1}$ flips, so the Hasse diagram of the higher Bruhat order $B(n,n-3)$ has $n(n+5)2^{n-4}$ edges. Lean proves the count in the consistent-set model, and Ziegler's theorem supplies the link to tilings. [Proof](problems/stephan-oeis-conjectures/proof/A060621.txt) · [Lean theorem](problems/stephan-oeis-conjectures/ZonotopeFlips/Total.lean#L249) |

| [Contextuality of real Pauli quadrics](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/) | Li, Gupta and Ganesh asked for a conceptual proof of the four-qubit geometry and for the limiting violated fraction. The five-qubit degree is **6,975**, and the general-rank violated fraction tends to **1/2**. [Four-qubit theorem](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/lean/Quadric/ClaimB.lean#L59) · [Five-qubit theorem](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/lean/Quadric/Five/Exact.lean#L45) · [Kernel replay](problems/pauli-quadric-contextuality/logs/claim-c-final-replay.txt) |
